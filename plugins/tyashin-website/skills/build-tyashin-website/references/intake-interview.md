# Intake interview: question bank

Ask in small batches of 2 to 4 questions, conversationally. Skip anything the owner already told you. Skip whatever doesn't fit their archetype: don't ask a physiotherapist about shipping, or a manufacturer about opening hours, unless it matters.

Each answer maps to an intake field (in brackets). Anything else the owner states as fact, such as a quote, a number or a year, goes **verbatim** into `confirmedFacts`.

## Always

- What's the business called, exactly as it should appear on the site? [`businessName`]
- In a sentence or two, what do you do or sell? [`offerings`]
- Who are your best customers? Who is the site for? [`audience`]
- Where do you work, or which areas do you serve? [`locations`]
- How should people reach you? Phone, WhatsApp, email, a physical address? Which one should the main button use? [`contact.*`]
- Do you have a website today? If so, what should the new one do better? [`existingWebsite`, `mustHaves`]
- How should it feel? Three words, any colours you love or hate, and sites you admire. [`style.mood`, `style.colors`, `style.references`]
- Anything to avoid? [`avoid`]
- What language should the site be in? [`language`]

## local-service (salon, clinic, physio, gym, tutor, trades, lodging)

- Your services: the main ones, and anything people often ask about. [`offerings`]
- Opening hours? [`contact.hours`]
- Do people book, call, WhatsApp or just walk in? [main CTA; `contact.*`]
- Any qualifications, registrations or memberships you'd like shown? Exact wording, please. [`confirmedFacts`]
- Prices: do you want them on the site? If yes, the exact prices. [`confirmedFacts`]
- Real reviews or testimonials you have permission to use, word for word, with the person's name or initials? [`confirmedFacts`]

## b2b-catalogue (manufacturer, supplier, wholesaler)

- Your product ranges or categories, and what sets each apart. [`offerings`]
- Who buys from you: trade, contractors, retailers, export? [`audience`]
- How do buyers enquire: quote request, WhatsApp, email, phone? [main CTA]
- Certifications, standards, capacities, years established? Only if you can state them exactly. [`confirmedFacts`]
- A catalogue PDF or spec sheets? (List them in asset debt if they're still coming.)

## marketing (software, agency, consultant, creator)

- The problem you solve, for whom, in their words. [`offerings`, `audience`]
- What the main action is: book a call, start a trial, contact us? [`mustHaves`]
- Pricing or plans to show? Exact figures only. [`confirmedFacts`]
- Customers or logos you have permission to name? [`confirmedFacts`]

## ecommerce (shop)

- What you sell, your main categories and your best sellers. [`offerings`]
- Explain: this free flow publishes the brand and brochure pages now. The online-store pages (products, cart, checkout) are set up afterwards from the dashboard.

## Closing the intake

Write back three lists: **confirmed facts**, **what I'll describe in my own words**, and **placeholders you still owe me**. Get a yes before you design.
